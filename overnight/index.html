Overnight Stays

$60 a night. Pull in, plug in, rest up.

Running 287 between Amarillo and the Metroplex? Book your nights online, pay when you book, and pull straight onto a paved full-hookup site in Memphis, Texas.

  • 20/30/50 amp power, water and sewer at the pad
  • Paved 25′ × 75′ site — big rigs and a tow car fit
  • Free laundry — washers and dryers at no charge
  • Fenced dog yard
  • Park-wide Wi-Fi, restrooms and hot showers
  • Right off US 287 at SH 256 — easy in, easy out

Book and pay

$60.00 + $4.95 tax = $64.95

You'll pay on Square's secure checkout and enter your arrival date and RV details there. Your receipt is your confirmation.

Staying longer than 7 nights? Call 806-460-2424. Staying a month or more? Sign the monthly agreement.
