Overnight Stay Terms

Effective November 23, 2026

These Overnight Stay Terms (the "Terms") are an agreement between Hall County Industrial Park, LLC d/b/a Foundry RV Park, a Texas limited liability company ("Foundry," "we" or "us"), and the person who books and pays for an overnight stay ("Guest" or "you"). By checking the box on our booking page, completing payment, or occupying a site, you agree to these Terms on behalf of yourself and everyone in your party. Stays of 30 nights or more require our monthly site use agreement instead.

1. What you are booking

You are booking a revocable, short-term license to park one recreational vehicle ("RV") on one RV site at 2100 N Greenwood Street, Memphis, Texas 79245 (the "Park") for the nights you pay for, and to use the Park's common amenities. This is a license, not a lease or tenancy, and it does not create any landlord-tenant relationship or any interest in real property.

We assign your site when you arrive. We may move you to a comparable site if needed for maintenance, safety or operations.

2. Rate and payment

The overnight rate is $60.00 per site per night, plus any applicable taxes, for one RV and up to the number of occupants it is designed to sleep. Full payment is due at booking through our online payment page. Your payment receipt is your confirmation.

The rate includes water, sewer, 20/30/50 amp electric, park Wi-Fi, free laundry and the dog yard. Covered or uncovered storage and potable water to take with you are sold separately.

Stays of up to 29 consecutive nights may be booked at the overnight rate. A stay of 30 nights or more requires the monthly site use agreement.

3. Check-in and check-out

Check-in is after 1:00 p.m. and check-out is by 11:00 a.m. Office hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily. If you will arrive after hours, call 806-460-2424 before you arrive and we will direct you to your site.

If you stay past check-out without paying for another night, you will be charged for an additional night. If you remain after your paid stay ends without our consent, your license ends and you must leave; we may charge three times (3x) the nightly rate for each extra day and may have the RV removed at your expense as permitted by Texas law.

4. Cancellations and refunds

Cancel at least 24 hours before 1:00 p.m. on your arrival date for a full refund. Cancellations made later than that, no-shows and early departures are not refunded. To cancel, call 806-460-2424 or email contact@foundryrvpark.com with the name on the booking.

If we cannot provide a site for a night you paid for, we will refund that night in full. That refund is our only obligation.

5. Your RV and your party

You must be at least 18 years old to book. You are responsible for everyone in your party and every visitor you bring, and for any damage they cause to the site, utilities or Park property.

Your RV must be roadworthy and self-contained, registered and insured as required by Texas law. Tents, car camping and sleeping outside an RV are not allowed. Only one RV per site unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Connect only to the pedestal at your site, use a proper sewer hose with a sealed fitting, and leave the site as clean as you found it.

6. Park rules

Drive 5 mph or slower in the Park. Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.; generators may not run during quiet hours.

Pets are welcome. Keep them leashed outside your RV and the dog yard, pick up after them, and do not leave them outside unattended. You are responsible for your pet's behavior and any harm it causes. We may require removal of an aggressive or disruptive animal.

No open fires except in approved containers where permitted, no fireworks, no illegal activity and no commercial activity from the site. Keep the Park's laundry and dog yard clean for the next guest.

We may end your stay immediately, without refund, if you or anyone in your party threatens the safety or peace of others, damages property, or seriously or repeatedly breaks these rules.

7. Liability and insurance

You use the Park, its amenities, utilities and Wi-Fi at your own risk. We are not responsible for loss of or damage to your RV, vehicles, pets or personal property, or for injury to anyone in your party, except to the extent caused by our gross negligence or willful misconduct. We do not guarantee uninterrupted utilities or Wi-Fi.

You agree to indemnify and hold harmless Foundry and its owners, managers and employees from claims, damages and costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from your stay or the acts of your party, pets or visitors, except to the extent caused by our gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Our insurance does not cover your RV or belongings. We recommend that your RV policy include liability coverage at least equal to Texas minimums.

8. General

These Terms, the Park rules posted at the Park and your payment receipt are the entire agreement for your overnight stay. If any part of these Terms is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. Texas law governs, and venue for any dispute is in Hall County, Texas.

We may update these Terms for future bookings; the version posted when you book applies to that stay.
