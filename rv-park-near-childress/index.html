Near Childress

An RV park 29 miles up the road from Childress.

Foundry RV Park is new, full hookup, and sits at 2100 N Greenwood Street in Memphis, Texas — straight up US 287 from Childress, about half an hour door to door. Thirty sites are open now for water and sewer camping, with electric hookups on by October 15, 2026.

Why look outside Childress

Same work, different town, brand-new park.

Childress has had a hard year on housing. Rents have climbed steeply as the data center campuses staff up, and the city has been working through a water supply problem since early September, with a citywide boil-water notice and Stage 4 restrictions in place.

Memphis is a separate city on a separate municipal water system, 29 miles northwest, and the park here is new from the ground up. For anybody working the Childress campuses, it is a half-hour commute to a site that was poured this year.

  • Perimeter drive lanes, so you pull through instead of three-point turning
  • Laundry on site, park-wide Wi-Fi, fenced pet area
  • $1,000 a month with water, electric, sewer and Wi-Fi included

One flat monthly rate per site, month to month. Water, electric, sewer and park-wide Wi-Fi are included — there is no separate utility bill at the end of the month.
