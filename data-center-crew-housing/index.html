Crew Housing

Three campuses. One place to put your crew.

Foundry RV Park is a brand-new 30-site, full-hookup park at 2100 N Greenwood Street in Memphis, Texas — inside 45 road miles of three data center campuses under construction in the Texas Panhandle. Open now for water and sewer camping, with electric hookups on by October 15, 2026.

Why crews end up here

Room for the rig, and a bed that is not two hours away.

Housing near this work is tight. Rents in Childress have climbed sharply as the campuses staff up, and the town has been working through a water supply problem since early September. Foundry is a different town on a different municipal system, about 29 miles up US 287, and every site in the park is new.

The layout was built for working rigs rather than weekend campers. One size of site, all of them big, with drive lanes on both outside edges so nobody is backing into an awning at the end of a twelve-hour shift.

  • 25′ × 75′ sites — a 40′ unit parks with the truck beside it, not behind it
  • 30-amp and 50-amp metered pedestals, potable water and sewer at every pad
  • Level, all-weather asphalt throughout — no gravel dust and no mud in March
  • Laundry on site, park-wide Wi-Fi, and a fenced pet area
  • Blocks of sites with monthly billing and one point of contact
  • Straight in off US 287 — no town driving with a fifth wheel behind you

For superintendents and commissioning leads

If you tow your own unit, this is the easy part — pick your dates, sign the agreement online from your phone, and pull in. Most travelling supervisors are working against a lodging per diem, and a monthly site typically leaves a good part of that in your pocket rather than a hotel's.

For general contractors and staffing firms

Tell us headcount and dates and we will hold sites. Monthly billing, one invoice, one phone number. If your people do not own rigs, talk to us anyway — we are working with trailer rental fleets on units set on our pads, and we would rather solve it than send you down the road.

Questions we get

Straight answers.

What does a site cost?
$1,000 a month, with water, electric, sewer and Wi-Fi included. First month plus a last-month deposit is due at signing, so $2,000 up front. Rigs with more than two air conditioners add $150 a month. Call 806-460-2424 about block pricing for a crew.
How long can we stay?
Month to month with no fixed end date — the park was built for multi-month assignments. Nightly and weekly stays are quoted by phone.
Do you take company billing?
Yes — monthly invoicing to the company, with a single point of contact for the block.
Can you handle a whole crew at once?
Thirty sites at opening and room on the property to build well past 200 as the work ramps. Ask early and we will reserve what you need.
Pets?
Pet friendly throughout, with a fenced run.
